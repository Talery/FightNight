# Asset credits

## FightNight biome backgrounds

Original biome backgrounds in `src/assets/biomes/` were generated for this project with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool, then cropped to 1600×900 and converted to RGB WebP.

- Art direction and selection: FightNight project.
- Generated sets: `catacombs`, `salt`, `citadel`, `marsh`, `monastery`, `mines`, `coast`, `garden`.
- Generated boss portraits: `debt-judge`, `salt-matron`, `ash-king`, `mire-mouth`, `bell-father`, `raven-duke`, `chain-sister`, `salt-giant`, `ash-bride`, `mire-abbot`, `bell-warden`, `raven-seer`; chroma-key sources were converted to transparent 512×768 lossless WebP.
- Generated unique relic icons: eight base relic designs in `src/assets/relics/`; chroma-key sources were converted to transparent 256×256 lossless WebP.
- Generated event category backgrounds in `src/assets/events/`; images were cropped to 1600×900 RGB WebP and selected through the event category registry.
- Original synthesized music loops in `src/assets/audio/` were generated locally for FightNight from deterministic oscillator layers; no third-party audio samples were used.
- No third-party source asset was used as an image input.
- Generated equipment and consumable illustrations: 13 files in `src/assets/items-v2/`, prepared as WebP for the item registry.
- Generated talent presentation: `src/assets/talents/ashen-sigil-tree.webp` and three interface frames in `src/assets/ui/`.

The generated FightNight assets above are project originals made through OpenAI image generation and local image processing. They do not incorporate third-party source images.

## FightNight — Dark Fighters

Six hero and six enemy combat figures used in `src/assets/dark-fighters/`.

- Original images created specifically for FightNight with OpenAI image generation.
- Locally processed into transparent, optimized WebP assets; no external character pack or third-party samples are used.
- Final prompt family: grounded painterly dark-fantasy adult combatants, semi-realistic anatomy, worn iron/leather, oppressive dungeon lighting; explicitly excluding cartoon, chibi, cute, childlike and toy-like styling.
